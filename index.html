hi, i'm shakshi.

i'm a software engineer with a soft spot for design. i build web things that feel hand-made, and work like they were engineered.

see what washed ashore open to new projects

the person behind the tide

i grew up believing the best things are made slowly, by hand, and then shipped fast.

most days i'm writing frontends and the services behind them: turning messy problems into interfaces people actually enjoy using. i care about the small stuff, like the easing on a button, the copy in an empty state, the one query that makes a page slow.

outside the editor you'll find me sketching in a notebook, collecting shells i will never do anything with, and arguing that chai is a personality.

before you ask…

when can you start?

sooner than you think. tell me what you're building and i'll tell you honestly when i can be useful, usually within a couple of weeks.

are you a designer or a developer?

a developer who refuses to hand off the last 10%. i write the code, but i'll also notice your button is two pixels off and quietly fix it.

what kind of work do you love?

products real people touch every day: web apps, interactive pages, design systems, tools that make a team faster. bonus points if it needs a little delight.

what won't you do?

dark patterns, fake countdown timers, and anything that makes a "cancel subscription" button harder to find than the beach on a cloudy day.

how do you like to work?

small, frequent, visible. i'd rather show you a rough thing on tuesday than a perfect thing never. async-friendly, with a call when it actually helps.

your biggest weakness?

i will absolutely spend an evening making a wave crash across a loading screen. you are looking at the evidence.

send a message in a bottle

write me a note, cork it, and throw it out to sea. it lands in my inbox, and i reply within a couple of days.
