A note from my roommate

Give me a problem. I'll probably come back with a product.

i'm not big on the whole "that's not my department" thing. i'll question the idea, obsess over the design, get my hands dirty with the code, and probably find three more things to improve along the way. i like making things work. i like making them look good. and i REALLY like when they do both.
